Portfolio

Three one-page WordPress builds

One brief, taken in three different directions by Theodor-Gabriel Nae. Real Gutenberg block themes, hand-written CSS, and every graphic on the three pages drawn for them.

The three companies are invented for this portfolio, so nothing on the pages is a claim about a real business.
