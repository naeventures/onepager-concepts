The practice

Halewick works with owner managed companies on the decisions that cannot be taken twice: selling the company, handing it to whoever holds it next, splitting up a business that has quietly become two, or bringing in the first senior person from outside the family.

One person does the work, which is the point and also the limit. Halewick takes few engagements at a time and says so before the first meeting rather than after it, and there is no team behind the name to hand the work to once it starts.

It will not be the third opinion brought in to outvote the first two, and it will not advise both sides of the same table, which happens more often in owner managed deals than it should. There is no auction to run here, and no fee that depends on one.

Founder

Nathaniel Osei Brandt founded Halewick Advisory to work on the decisions an owner only makes once: selling, handing the company to whoever holds it next, splitting up a business that has quietly become two, or bringing in the first senior person from outside the family.

He sorts by decision rather than by sector, on the view that an owner deciding whether to sell the company their father started is not thinking about manufacturing. They are thinking about whether they will regret it. Most of the work is questions, not spreadsheets, and some of it is telling someone the offer in front of them is fine and their reluctance is not about the money.

He will not take a brief where the timetable is already fixed and the job is to make it look considered, and he will not advise both sides of the same table. He works alone for now, which is a stage rather than a philosophy: whoever joins him later will meet the client before the engagement does.
